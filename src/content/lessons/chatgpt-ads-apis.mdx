## How to read the internal tools in our examples

OpenAI describes how its system finds, checks and ranks ads before an auction. The exact messages those parts exchange are private. Names like prepare\_queries, embed and final\_gate belong to our example of how a system like this might work. [OpenAI engineering account](https://openai.com/index/building-chatgpt-ad-stack/)

<aside class="lesson-note">

### What the reporting case can answer

How often were your ads shown and how much did you spend in the campaigns and dates you chose? Conversation details, relevance scores and the reasons for individual auction results stay inside the platform.
